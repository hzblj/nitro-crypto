## 0.3.1

### Patch Changes

- [#18](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/pull/18) [`b73ed73`](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/commit/b73ed7358702d4a4e8f6136756bc215c895f2b81) Thanks [@hzblj](https://github.com/hzblj)! - `decodePsbt` reads a PSBT whose outputs exceed its inputs instead of refusing it, so a wallet can show an unfinished one such as a marketplace offer (a seller's coin and asking price, signed `SIGHASH_SINGLE | ANYONECANPAY`, waiting for a buyer's inputs). The new `balance` is the inputs less the outputs, negative for such a PSBT, whose `fee` is then `undefined`. `signPsbt` and `finalizePsbt` still refuse it: this package signs only `SIGHASH_ALL` (or Taproot's default), which could never complete it.

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- [#13](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/pull/13) [`55e2af9`](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/commit/55e2af9bc1a3a385dc83118ef29a1e63c3147b39) Thanks [@hzblj](https://github.com/hzblj)! - A watch-only account sync, in a subpath of its own: `@hzblj/crypto-bitcoin/sync`. `createBitcoinSync` follows an account from its extended public key (the engine's `wallet.extendedPublicKey`, or a signer's xpub, tpub, zpub or vpub through `parseExtendedKey`), finding its receiving and change addresses up to the gap limit (20), or one key's address, or an address alone. It reports the balance (confirmed, unconfirmed, total, spendable), the coins with their paths and confirmations, the history with each transaction's effect on the account (received, sent, self, fee), and the next receiving and change addresses. A sync reads only what can have changed and resumes from a plain JSON snapshot (`sync.snapshot()`, bigints as strings) an app persists; it follows reorganizations, confirmations, dropped and replaced transactions, and heals when the backend's history and its summaries disagree. Payments use the adapter's coin selection over trusted coins and become one PSBT for coins of any addresses of the account, with every key's origin and each coin's previous transaction (checked against the coin), for the engine (`sync.signPsbt`) or an air-gapped signer; fee bumps follow BIP-125, and `broadcast` records what it sends at once. The backend is a `BitcoinBackend`: `esploraBackend` speaks Esplora (mempool.space by default, any electrs by `url`) with timeouts, `AbortSignal`, retries with backoff on 429 and 5xx, bounded concurrency and pacing: public servers (mempool.space, blockstream.info) are asked at most two requests a second, two at once, an app's own server fifty, and a server that cannot be reached several times in a row is left alone for a while (`UNREACHABLE`). Requests go through the global `fetch` or any client the app passes (`fetch` from `react-native-nitro-fetch`, or a `SyncFetch` of its own: a URL and `{method, headers, body, signal}` in, `{ok, status, headers, text}` out), and one that ignores the signal still never has more than `concurrency` requests running. Any other source of chain data plugs in as a `BitcoinBackend`. Every failure is a `SyncError` with a stable code. Public Esplora servers rate-limit and block busy IP addresses: a wallet with real users should run or rent its own and pass its `url`. The main entry has no network code. Accounts are native SegWit (P2WPKH, BIP-84) or Taproot (P2TR, BIP-86: an xpub or tpub at `m/86'/…` with `scriptType: 'p2tr'`, as Taproot has no SLIP-132 prefix), and a Taproot payment's PSBT carries BIP-371's internal keys and derivations for the engine's Schnorr signatures (`signPsbt` derives each key with its type). Tested on the engine as WebAssembly against a fake Esplora, @scure/bip32, @scure/btc-signer and BIP-84's vectors.

- [#14](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/pull/14) [`dd905b8`](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/commit/dd905b8142ef7a939ddb67b8564977ae7a4626b5) Thanks [@hzblj](https://github.com/hzblj)! - Taproot accounts and message signing. `deriveAccount({chain: 'bitcoin', addressType: 'p2tr'})` is a BIP-86 account at `m/86'/{coin}'/{account}'/{change}/{index}` with a Bech32m address, its key tweaked inside the engine; P2WPKH stays the default, and `{path}` alone is still P2WPKH. `watchAccount` and `addressFromPublicKey` take `addressType: 'p2tr'` too (an x-only key or SEC1). Taproot accounts plan payments at 57.5 vB an input and sign them over BIP-341 with SIGHASH_DEFAULT. PSBTs gain BIP-371's fields: Taproot accounts write `PSBT_IN_TAP_INTERNAL_KEY` and `PSBT_IN_TAP_BIP32_DERIVATION` (and the output ones on change) and sign `PSBT_IN_TAP_KEY_SIG`, also for a coin of their key under a script tree whose `PSBT_IN_TAP_MERKLE_ROOT` is given; `decodePsbt` reports them (`taprootInternalKey`, `taprootMerkleRoot`, `taprootDerivations`, `taprootKeySignature`); and `finalizePsbt` finalizes P2WPKH and Taproot key path inputs together, from any accounts, verifying every signature on the engine, including inputs a signer already finalized, which it used to refuse. Accounts gain `signMessage({message, format?})` (BIP-322 simple, prefixed `smp` by default, `bip322-unprefixed`, or `legacy` BIP-137 for P2WPKH) and `verifyMessage`, and the chain `verifyMessage({address, message, signature})`, which also reads legacy signatures for P2PKH and P2SH-P2WPKH and says `UNSUPPORTED` for what needs a script interpreter. `features.messageSigning` and `features.taproot` are now true, and `formats` lists `bech32m-p2tr`, `p2tr-bip341`, `bip322-simple` and `bip137`. Taproot needs an engine with `secp256k1-schnorr` and `taproot-tweak`, which the adapter does not require: on an engine without them it registers, P2WPKH works, and Taproot throws `UNSUPPORTED`. Held to BIP-86's and BIP-341's vectors, every BIP-322 vector, Bitcoin Core's message vector, `@scure/btc-signer` (sighashes of every type, transactions and PSBTs byte for byte) and `@noble/curves`.

  Type changes to check when upgrading: `BitcoinWatchAccount.addressType` is now `'p2wpkh' | 'p2tr'` (it was `'p2wpkh'`), so code that assumed P2WPKH should narrow on it; `BitcoinPsbtInput` and `BitcoinPsbtOutput` have the new Taproot fields, which code building those objects itself must add.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- [#4](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/pull/4) [`219ccf7`](https://github.com/hzblj/nitro-crypto/commit/219ccf7ef493c3a89854d4fdc500568e578cfabc) Thanks [@hzblj](https://github.com/hzblj)! - PSBTs (BIP-174, version 0) and watch-only accounts. `crypto.chains.bitcoin.watchAccount({publicKey, path, fingerprint})` is an account of a key held elsewhere, and `watchAccount({extendedKey, path, fingerprint, change?, index?})` any address of an account whose extended public key a signer shared (`derivePublic`, on an engine with public derivation): its address, `createTransaction`, and `createPsbt(plan, {previousTransactions?})`, which writes each input's coin and BIP-32 derivation, the derivation on change, and each coin's previous transaction when given. Every account is one too, now with its wallet's `fingerprint`, and signs with `signPsbt(psbt)` (the inputs that spend its coins, SIGHASH_ALL only). `decodePsbt` reads a PSBT strictly, with each input's address, value and whether that value can be checked, and the fee; `finalizePsbt` verifies every signature on the engine before it extracts the transaction. The adapter now states `psbt: true`. Parity with `@scure/btc-signer` both ways.
