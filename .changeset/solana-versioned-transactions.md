Versioned transactions, off-chain messages and a @solana/kit signer, so a wallet can sign what modern Solana dApps send. `account.signTransaction` and `chain.compileMessage` take `version: 0` and `addressLookupTables`, and compile a v0 message that loads accounts from the tables as kit's `compressTransactionMessageUsingAddressLookupTables` and compiler do, byte for byte. `decodeMessage` reads legacy and v0 messages and names looked-up accounts from the tables' addresses when given (`{addressLookupTables}`), `decodeTransaction` reads a wire transaction with its signature slots, and `account.partiallySignTransaction` signs the account's own slot of a transaction others sign too. `account.signOffchainMessage`, `chain.encodeOffchainMessage`, `decodeOffchainMessage` and `verifyOffchainMessage` handle Solana's off-chain messages, versions 0 and 1, encoded as kit encodes them. `@hzblj/crypto-solana/kit` exports `toKitSigner(account)`, a kit `TransactionPartialSigner` and `MessagePartialSigner`; `@solana/kit` is an optional peer dependency that only this entry names, for its types. `features.versionedTransactions` and `features.offchainMessages` are now `true`. Changed: a decoded account's `address` is `undefined` for a v0 account loaded from a table that was not supplied (`DecodedSolanaAccount`, `DecodedSolanaInstruction`), decoded messages say their `version` and `addressTableLookups`, and compiling refuses what kit refuses (more than 64 accounts, 12 signers or 64 instructions) for legacy messages too.
