A watch-only account sync, in a subpath of its own: `@hzblj/crypto-bitcoin/sync`. `createBitcoinSync` follows an account from its extended public key (the engine's `wallet.extendedPublicKey`, or a signer's xpub, tpub, zpub or vpub through `parseExtendedKey`), finding its receiving and change addresses up to the gap limit (20), or one key's address, or an address alone. It reports the balance (confirmed, unconfirmed, total, spendable), the coins with their paths and confirmations, the history with each transaction's effect on the account (received, sent, self, fee), and the next receiving and change addresses. A sync reads only what can have changed and resumes from a plain JSON snapshot (`sync.snapshot()`, bigints as strings) an app persists; it follows reorganizations, confirmations, dropped and replaced transactions, and heals when the backend's history and its summaries disagree. Payments use the adapter's coin selection over trusted coins and become one PSBT for coins of any addresses of the account, with every key's origin and each coin's previous transaction (checked against the coin), for the engine (`sync.signPsbt`) or an air-gapped signer; fee bumps follow BIP-125, and `broadcast` records what it sends at once. The backend is a `BitcoinBackend`: `esploraBackend` speaks Esplora (mempool.space by default, any electrs by `url`) with timeouts, `AbortSignal`, retries with backoff on 429 and 5xx, bounded concurrency and pacing: public servers (mempool.space, blockstream.info) are asked at most two requests a second, two at once, an app's own server fifty, and a server that cannot be reached several times in a row is left alone for a while (`UNREACHABLE`). Requests go through the global `fetch` or any client the app passes (`fetch` from `react-native-nitro-fetch`, or a `SyncFetch` of its own: a URL and `{method, headers, body, signal}` in, `{ok, status, headers, text}` out), and one that ignores the signal still never has more than `concurrency` requests running. Any other source of chain data plugs in as a `BitcoinBackend`. Every failure is a `SyncError` with a stable code. Public Esplora servers rate-limit and block busy IP addresses: a wallet with real users should run or rent its own and pass its `url`. The main entry has no network code. Taproot accounts (P2TR) are reserved for a later version. Tested on the engine as WebAssembly against a fake Esplora, @scure/bip32, @scure/btc-signer and BIP-84's vectors.
